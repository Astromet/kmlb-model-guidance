Combined bulletin

All four models in MOS layout, one row per model under each element. Temperatures more than 3°F from the other models' median are highlighted. Rain and thunder chances are shaded by value.

  • X/N max (at 00Z) and min (at 12Z), °F. CLD CL FW SC BK OV. WDR tens of degrees. WSP, GST knots.
  • P06, P12 chance of at least .01 in, percent. For ECMWF this is the share of its 51 ensemble members reaching .05 in, since the raw members drizzle a few hundredths almost every period.
  • Q06, Q12 QPF category: 0 none, 1 .01–.09, 2 .10–.24, 3 .25–.49, 4 .50–.99, 5 1.00–1.99, 6 2.00+ in.
  • T06, T12 thunder chance in percent; MOS also shows the severe chance after the slash. CAP ECMWF max CAPE, hundreds of J/kg; it stands in for thunder, which ECMWF's open data lacks.
  • CIG ceiling category 1–8 and VIS visibility category 1–7, as in MOS. ECMWF has neither.
  • GFS and NAM are MOS guidance; NBM is the National Blend; ECMWF is raw IFS model output, not statistically corrected.
